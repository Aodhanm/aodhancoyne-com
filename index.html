The Author

Aodhan Coyne

I work on the history of early California, from 1769 to the American conquest, and on making the archives of that period usable. My principal project is Archives of California, a document-level calendar of the Savage transcripts at the Bancroft Library, the copies that remain after the originals were destroyed in 1906. I am extending it beyond Savage to the other nineteenth-century compilers who worked from the same records, among them Hittell and Eldredge, and cross-referencing it with the pueblo archives of San José and Los Angeles, with further collections to follow. The aim is a cross-archive index of early California, one that lets a person or a document be followed across collections. Alongside it I maintain a register of the Spanish and Mexican land grants, a registry of ship arrivals to the Californias, and a series of historical maps. All of it is published openly and added to continually. B.A. in History, University of California, Berkeley (2025).

A diseño is the hand-drawn sketch map a grantee filed as evidence of his claim. These were submitted to the United States land commission after 1851 and survive in the Bancroft land-case files.
